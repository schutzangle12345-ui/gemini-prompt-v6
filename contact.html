Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to confirm a jet or yacht?

For peak dates, we recommend booking villas 6-12 months in advance. Private jets can often be confirmed within 2-4 hours, while yacht charters typically require 2-7 days to finalize contracts and APA deposits.

What information should I prepare before asking for a quote?

To provide the most accurate pricing, please have your dates, number of passengers (including pets), preferred luggage capacity for jets, and any specific dietary or crew requirements ready.

How do you handle changes to my itinerary?

One message to BookLux handles everything. We coordinate with the jet operator, the villa manager, and the chauffeur team to synchronize your new schedule, avoiding multi-vendor confusion and unnecessary fees.

Are there red flags when booking high-value services online?

Always verify the operator's certificates and insurance. We pre-vet every provider on our platform to ensure they meet international safety standards and have transparent cancellation policies.
